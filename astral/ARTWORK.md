# Original anime portraits

Four 1024×1536 PNG portraits were created with the **built-in image_gen tool** and copied into `public/astral/art/`. Real alpha transparency is preserved. All subjects are original, clearly adult, fully clothed, and non-explicit. The previous scenery-heavy illustrations and the four removed characters are no longer shipped.

Shared prompt direction: Japanese TV anime / visual-novel character sprite; crisp thin dark linework, solid colors and two-tone cel shading; expressive stylized anime eyes, simplified features, mature adult proportions. Portrait 2:3, mid-thigh-up, head and shoulders in frame, character nearly fills the canvas. Genuine transparent alpha background; no environment, scenery, furniture, particles, text, watermark, frame, photorealism, painterly texture, 3D, or chibi treatment.
